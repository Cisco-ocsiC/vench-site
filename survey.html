Site survey & as-built package

We survey the route or site before design, and document exactly what was built afterwards.

Know the site before you design, and prove what was built

Good design starts with an accurate picture of the ground, the building and the existing assets. A complete as-built package then gives the owner the records they need to operate and maintain the network for years.

We can provide either on its own, or as part of an end-to-end project together with our design services.

Enquire about survey and as-built

Site survey

Surveys for routes, exchanges, data centres and equipment sites.

Desktop study

Review of existing assets and records, Dial Before You Dig data, aerial imagery and GIS before anyone goes on site.

Route survey

Field survey of the proposed route by walking or driving it: GPS positions, photos, road, rail and river crossings, existing pits and ducts, access and obstacles.

Exchange and data centre survey

Space, power and cooling, cable entry points, pathway and tray capacity, ODF and rack availability, and rack elevations.

Survey deliverables: survey report with photos, marked-up drawings or maps, constraints and risks, a recommended route or option, and a preliminary bill of quantities.

Technical approach

The detail behind a tidy site.

Fibre and connectors

OS2 single-mode and OM3, OM4 or OM5 multimode as the application needs. LC duplex for standard links, and MPO trunks for parallel optics at 40G, 100G and above, with the polarity method chosen to suit the TIA-568.3 standard.

Frame and panel layout

A clear hierarchy from the optical distribution frame (ODF) through to equipment racks, with ODF-to-ODF tie cables run on planned paths, and spare capacity left for growth.

Pathways and separation

Separate A and B pathways for resilience, fibre kept apart from power, planned ladder and tray fill, and cable bend radius protected at every turn.

Labelling and records

A consistent labelling scheme and cable schedule, based on ANSI/TIA-606 administration practice, so every tie cable can be traced from both ends.

Testing

Loss, length and polarity testing in line with AS/NZS 14763.3, with OTDR testing where the design calls for it, and results handed over in the as-built package.

Standards we work to

AS/NZS 3084 for telecommunications pathways and spaces, the AS/NZS ISO/IEC 11801 cabling series, and your own operator standards and specifications.

Fibre loss calculator

Estimate the end-to-end loss of a single-mode fibre link, and the margin against your equipment.

Typical values: 0.35 dB/km at 1310 nm and 0.22 dB/km at 1550 nm, 0.05 to 0.1 dB per fusion splice, 0.3 to 0.5 dB per connector pair. Use the figures from your cable and equipment datasheets for final design.

DWDM link budget calculator

Check span loss and optical signal-to-noise ratio (OSNR) for a chain of amplified spans.

OSNR (0.1 nm) = 58 + Pch − span loss − NF − 10·log10(N spans). This is the standard estimate for identical, fully loss-compensated spans at 1550 nm; it ignores fibre nonlinearity and ROADM or filter penalties. Take the required OSNR from the vendor's datasheet for your transponder and rate. Use it for early planning, not as a final design.

Take-home pay calculator (Australia)

Work out pay before or after tax, shown weekly, fortnightly, monthly and yearly.

Estimate for Australian residents who claim the tax-free threshold. Uses the ATO resident tax rates. It does not include super, HELP or other debts, offsets, the low-income Medicare levy reduction or the Medicare levy surcharge. Check your own situation at ato.gov.au.

Public holidays in Australia

Date, occasion, and whether it applies in each state or territory.

Yes = applies. Part = part-day or only in certain areas (hover for details). – = does not apply. Checked every day against the Fair Work Ombudsman lists, so government changes are picked up automatically. Regional holidays vary, so check your state or territory's official list.

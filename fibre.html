Fibre infrastructure planning & design

Route planning and design for new, expanded and optimised fibre networks across Australia.

From first route to build-ready design

We plan and design fibre infrastructure for every stage of a network's life: building new routes, expanding existing infrastructure, and optimising existing networks by providing new route diversity.

Different environments need different thinking. We bring route planning and design experience across the full range of terrain and corridors that Australian networks cross, from long-haul routes between cities to dense, congested metro areas.

Routes we plan and design

Each environment has its own constraints, approvals and risks. We design with those in mind from the start.

Inter-city routes

Long-haul fibre between cities and major population centres, designed for resilience and for future capacity growth.

Coastal routes

Fibre planning along coastal corridors, taking into account the particular conditions and constraints of coastal environments.

Rail routes

Design of fibre along rail corridors, a common path for diverse, high-capacity routes.

Highway routes

Planning and design of fibre alongside highways and major roads, linking cities, towns and regions.

Regional networks

Fibre design to reach regional areas, connecting communities and sites outside the major cities.

Metro and congested networks

Design in dense urban areas where space in pits, ducts and conduits is limited, including new diversity for congested networks.

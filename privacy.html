Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

1. Overview

Vench (“we”, “us”) is an Australian fibre and optical network planning and design consultancy based in Sydney, New South Wales. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose and protect your personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

2. What we collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide our services and operate our website. This may include:

  • Your name, email address and phone number when you use the enquiry form
  • Your organisation name and project details when you engage us
  • Basic technical information (device type, browser, IP address) for site security and analytics

3. How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide a quote
  • Deliver the services you engage us for
  • Send you project updates and invoices
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with our legal and tax obligations

4. Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Our professional advisers (lawyers, accountants)
  • Government bodies where required by law
  • Service providers who help us operate (hosting, email delivery) — who are bound by confidentiality

5. Data storage

Personal information is stored on servers located in Australia. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information held by us. To make a request, email us at hello@vench.cc.

7. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy should be directed to us at hello@vench.cc or by phone at +61 2 9000 0000.

8. ABN verification

Our Australian Business Number can be verified on the Australian Business Register at abr.business.gov.au. Enter your ABN or our business name to confirm our registration details.
